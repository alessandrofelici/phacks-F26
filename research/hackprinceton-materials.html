Second Key: refined spec (cycle C2, 2026-11-09)

Track: Business + Enterprise (fraud prevention = cybersecurity/fintech, B2B for banks). Sponsor stack: Capital One Best Financial Hack (Nessie mock-bank API optional), Photon (family co-sign over iMessage).

R1 Facts for the pitch (checked)

  • FTC Protecting Older Consumers 2025-26 (Oct 2026): adults 60+ reported ~$3B lost in 2025, 5x since 2020; largest losses by bank transfer, then crypto; reports >$100K = 6% of reports but 70% of dollars.
  • Regulation is moving toward holds: FINRA filed (Federal Register, Sep 9 2026, SR-FINRA-2026-018) a new Rule 2166, a temporary delay of up to 5 business days for suspected fraud, for any customer, plus longer Rule 2165 holds (55 → 145 business days) and "emergency contact" wording for trusted contacts. Still a proposal, not approved as far as I found. Banks rely on state hold laws + the 2024 interagency statement.
  • McAfee 2026: 13% of parents have a family codeword.

R2 Tech

Need Choice Notes
Call screening Twilio Voice + ConversationRelay (text stream) → Claude classifier prompt + keyword rules Same plumbing as Neighbor Call. CallScreenBench's 4B model is optional and unreleased, so skip it.
Bank Capital One Nessie (customers, accounts, transfers) for the sponsor prize + our own "hold" layer api.nessieisreal.com is blocked from this machine (proxy), so it's unverified here; 2026 hackathon tracks still list it. Nessie has no hold concept, so our service sits in front: POST /transfer → risk check → held → co-sign → forward to Nessie. Fallback: an in-app mock bank.
Co-sign Photon Spectrum iMessage (proactive DM via im.space.create) with a reply "YES 4821 / NO" Twilio SMS fallback.
App Next.js: "Grandma's bank app" (big buttons) + "Family view" + "Bank ops console" (B2B angle)

R3 Prior art and the differentiator

  • EverSafe (AI monitoring of accounts/email, read-only trusted advocates, ~$7.49/mo; Fidelity integration 2026), Carefull (Edward Jones partnership Jun 2026; monitoring + caregiver alerts), True Link (prepaid card with merchant blocks + real-time purchase alerts), ElderGuard AI (hackathon winner 2025: deepfake call detection + family notification), Pixel/iOS call screening.
  • All of them either watch the call or watch the account, and alert after the fact. Differentiator: "Second Key links the two: when a pressure call is in progress, the next outgoing transfer is held until a family member co-signs, which is the 'temporary delay + trusted contact' model regulators are writing into FINRA Rule 2166, delivered as an API banks can plug in."
  • Creativity 3 → 4 with the call-to-payment link + regulatory hook.

R4 Design

Flow: call arrives on the senior's line (Twilio number) → live transcript → risk score per turn (rules: urgency, secrecy, "don't tell", gift card, crypto ATM, wire, bail, IRS; plus Claude judgment) → risk ≥ threshold: session flagged "pressure call active", family pinged ("Rosa is on a call that looks like a bail scam") → senior opens the bank app and tries a $4,800 transfer → held → family iMessage with amount, payee, call summary → family replies NO → transfer cancelled, senior sees a calm explanation; YES + code → released to Nessie. Safe-word step: during the flagged call the agent (whisper-only to the senior via SMS, or audible if the senior opts in) suggests asking the family question; we won't speak to the caller by default (consent, false positives).

The live transcript scrolls and the risk meter climbs; at turn 3, family gets an iMessage. - 0:55 The judge (as grandma) taps "Send $4,800" in the bank app → "Held: waiting for Ana". - 1:10 Family replies "NO". Legal/ethics notes for Q&A: two-party-consent states mean the screener announces itself; a family override on false positives; there's no credential access, since the senior initiates every transfer.

Neighbor Call: refined spec (cycle C1, 2026-11-09)

Track: Healthcare (public health). Sponsor stack: Photon (iMessage family alerts via Spectrum), Amazon Practical AI (generic).

R1 Facts for the pitch (checked)

  • Maricopa County 2025: 430 heat-related deaths; AC present in 94% of indoor deaths; people 50+ were 90% of indoor deaths (Maricopa 2025 Heat Deaths Report, via ABC15/search; PDF not opened, so the 94% vs "AC not working" split is unknown).
  • 15.8M Americans 65+ live alone (ACS 2024). Wildfire smoke ~41K deaths/yr (Qiu et al., Nature, Sep 2025).
  • CDC/NIA warning signs for older adults: confusion or acting strangely, no sweating, dizziness, nausea, headache, weakness, vomiting, fast/weak pulse; fans don't prevent heat illness in the high 90s (CDPH). These become the deterministic red-flag list.
  • No published evaluation of phone check-in programs during heat waves was found. Pitch it as filling a gap, not as "proven".

R3 Prior art and the differentiator

  • Daily AI check-in call services exist: AloneAssist ($19.99/mo, conversational AI call, alerts after 3 misses), Verocall ($19/mo), iamfine ($14.99, press-1), police "R U OK" programs. NYC "Be a Buddy" uses human volunteers.
  • Differentiator (one sentence): "Not a daily subscription: a free, event-triggered heat and smoke response that calls only when the weather turns dangerous, asks heat-specific clinical questions, and hands the county a ranked list of who needs a ride to a cooling center now."
  • Novelty is moderate (3/5); the strength is the public-health framing + the trigger + the coordinator view.

R4 Design

Flow: NWS/AirNow poll (or demo button) → select enrolled seniors in affected zones → Twilio outbound call → ConversationRelay ↔ our WebSocket ↔ Claude (script + free talk) → answers parsed into CheckIn → rules engine → status green/yellow/red → red: family iMessage via Photon + dashboard alert + offer cooling-center ride; no answer x2: family + "wellness check" flag.

ShiftProof: refined spec (cycle C3, 2026-11-09)

Track: Healthcare (fit 3). Sponsor: Photon (SMS/iMessage reminders).

R1 Facts (checked)

  • CMS-2454-IFC (Jun 1 2026): adults 19-64 in expansion; 80 h/month or $580/month earnings; exemptions include pregnant/postpartum, Tribal members, veterans with total disability rating, medically frail, caregivers (parents of children under 13; non-resident caregivers need 80 h of caregiving), people meeting SNAP/TANF work rules; short-term hardship. Self-attestation under penalty of perjury allowed through Dec 31 2027 when no electronic data exists.
  • Oct 29 2026: a federal court struck the IFR's extra "significantly impairs" test for medical frailty and enjoined it for the plaintiff states for 6 months (multi-state suit). Rules differ by state now.
  • Nebraska (live May 1 2026): applicants are checked on the month before applying; proof is uploaded at iServe.Nebraska.gov (QR code on the verification letter); school/apprenticeship needs the Individual Declaration Form; letters set due dates. Montana and Arkansas started Jul 2026, Michigan Oct 1 2026 for new applicants. California asked for a 6-month delay.

R3 Prior art (big change)

  • CMS "Emmy" (Eligibility Made Easy): an open-source, federally built applicant-facing app (Rails; github.com/DSACMS/iv-cbv-payroll, weekly releases, updated Nov 6 2026) that guides beneficiaries through reporting, connects to payroll/gig data via consent-based verification, and produces an "audit-ready evidence package" for the state. That is ShiftProof's core.
  • Vendors: Argyle (gig/payroll CBV for states, criticized for collecting credentials), Equifax Work Number (~$15/report), DiCIT (Digital Public Works). Consumer explainer sites: medicaidworkcheck.com, careroute.ai.
  • What's left for us: (1) people in states that haven't adopted Emmy/CBV, (2) cash/informal workers that payroll connectors can't see (screenshots, bank deposits, employer letters), (3) proactive month-by-month tracking + reminders before the look-back month, (4) the exemption screener (now volatile after the Oct 29 ruling).

R5 Score

Fit 3, pattern 4, feasibility 4, charity 5, creativity 2 (Emmy), sponsor +1 → 19 (was 21). Drop from the top 3. Keep it as a pitch slide fact if building Second Key or Neighbor Call ("benefits paperwork is the next frontier"). If the team wants this space anyway, build on Emmy (open source) rather than against it, and say so.
