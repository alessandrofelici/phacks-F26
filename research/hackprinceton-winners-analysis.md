## Sponsor prizes that recur
- **Photon**: F25 "Exploring Hybrid Intelligence", **5 winners** ($400 + $600 credits + interview fast-track for 1st; runner-ups $100 + $200): Messenger AI, PiggyBank, TextFly, Wings, doeve. S26 "Agents in iMessage" (cash): WaterShield, Wavelength. Photon is a Fall 2026 sponsor again → highest-odds sponsor prize. **Photon also appears in 4 of the 6 S26 track winners' stacks.**
- **Capital One** "Best Financial Hack": $1,000, 1 winner (F25 edgecart, which also used Knot). Wings used Nessie + Knot + iMessage kit.
- **Amazon** "Best Practical AI Innovation": $1,000 split over 3 winners (MindPad, Cropit, ViralWave).
- Knot API (purchase data) had 3+3 winners but isn't a Fall 2026 sponsor. Regeneron (clinical trials, ~11 winners in S26) isn't listed for Fall 2026 either.
- Stacking is normal: MindPad 3 prizes, PiggyBank 3, edgecart 3, Asclepius 3.

## Implications
1. Make **iMessage via Photon** the user interface of whatever we build: it's both a sponsor prize and a common thread in track winners.
2. Healthcare winners are real-time *monitor → alert* systems (VITAL) or predictive (Synovia). **Neighbor Call matches the VITAL pattern.**
3. B+E winners are agents that run a business workflow end to end (LEGR, Jigsaw). **Second Key pitched B2B to banks fits.** Capital One is 1 winner / $1,000.
4. Sustainability winners are geospatial/data or IoT (TerraLink, Watt's Up, WaterShield). **Bill Rescue as a consumer bill helper is a weak fit**; it needs a map/data layer (energy-burden by tract) to look like a winner.
5. Seniors + voice already won Education (Lume, F25), so judges respond to older-adult accessibility.
