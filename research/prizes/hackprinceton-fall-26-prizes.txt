HackPrinceton Fall '26 Devpost (https://hackprinceton-fall-26.devpost.com/), fetched 2026-11-13 (event day).
Submissions "open soon". 8 non-cash prizes listed, all MLH. Photon / Capital One / Amazon / other sponsor prizes are NOT on the page yet.

TRACKS (official wording, abridged)
- Healthcare: healthcare delivery, wellness, public health. e.g. telehealth, AI diagnostics, mental health, fitness tracking.
- Sustainability: environmental challenges or eco-friendly behavior. e.g. carbon tracking, smart waste management, renewable energy.
- Business & Enterprise: financial tools, business operations. e.g. fintech for startups, B2B billing, analytics dashboards, cybersecurity, privacy.
- Entertainment: creatively integrate entertainment and media. e.g. storytelling apps, interactive experiences, media literacy platforms.
- Education: learning more interactive, accessible or personalized. e.g. e-learning, immersive/virtual educational experiences.
- Science & Discovery: "turn curiosity into discovery". e.g. research accelerators, data models, tools to aid scientific processes and methods.

OVERALL
- Best Overall Hack: must excel on creativity, utility, charity, avidity. Track winners not eligible.
- Best Rookie Hack: first-time hackathon participants.
- Best Hardware Hack: IoT, robotics, wearables, physical computing.

REQUIREMENTS
1. Public GitHub repo created Nov 13-15 2026.
2. Optional video <= 2 min. In-person demos required Sunday.
3. Add Discord usernames to the submission.
